Our Philosophy

Why we combine technology with operational staff.

Most development agencies view their job as finished once code is committed to a repository. In reality, modern businesses don't just need software—they need the ongoing operational bandwidth to operate that software, manage customer communications, and execute day-to-day work.

Lintech solves this by providing both sides of the coin: our engineering team builds custom tools and web infrastructure, while our virtual assistant wing provides trained personnel to keep client operations moving without founder burnout.
